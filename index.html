Setting Up Anti-Freeze IPTV on Your Firestick 4K Max

Follow these simple steps. You need the Firestick 4K Max, a Wi-Fi or Ethernet connection, and a paid subscription.

  1. Turn on your Firestick and go to the home screen.
  2. Open the search icon and type the name of a supported player (for example, "IPTV Smarters Pro").
  3. Install the app from the Amazon App Store.
  4. Open the app and choose "Login with Xtream Codes" or "Add Playlist" – this depends on the player.
  5. Enter the URL, username, and password that SubTV IP gives you after purchase.
  6. In the player settings, set the buffer / cache to 5,000 ms. This matches the recommendation from the service to prevent ISP throttling.
  7. Optionally, change your DNS to Cloudflare 1.1.1.1 (in your network settings) for extra speed. The service suggests this to avoid throttling.
  8. Start watching. Your streams should load quickly and stay stable.
